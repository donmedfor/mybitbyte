## How I Study

My general approach for every certification:

1. **Read the official objectives** — map every domain to a note in Obsidian.
2. **Build a lab** — if it's practical, replicate the target environment locally (VMs, Docker, AD lab).
3. **Practice daily** — 30–60 min of hands-on drills beats 4 hours of passive video.
4. **Take notes in Markdown** — every command, every gotcha, every "aha" moment.
5. **Do practice exams** — treat them as real; review every wrong answer.
6. **Write it up** — the writeup you produce afterwards is worth more than the cert itself.
